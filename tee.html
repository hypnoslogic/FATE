Intra-operative points

  • Loading. Anaesthesia lowers preload and afterload, so MR and functional TR look less severe. Re-grade at representative blood pressure (eg phenylephrine challenge). AS gradients fall with low flow; use AVA and DI.
  • SAM risk before MV repair M. AL/PL ratio <1.3 and C-sept distance <25 mm. A small, hyperdynamic LV and a basal septal bulge add risk.
  • TV annulus E. ≥40 mm or >21 mm/m² (diastole, ME 4C): consider TV repair at left-sided valve surgery, even with less than severe TR.
  • Aortic atheroma K. Thickness ≥4 mm, ulcerated or mobile = complex, higher embolic risk. TEE cannot see the distal ascending aorta and proximal arch (tracheal blind spot); use epiaortic scanning before cannulation or clamping.
  • LAA. Emptying velocity <20 cm/s is associated with dense SEC and thrombus. Image the LAA in two planes before cardioversion or LAA procedures.
  • After bypass. Check for new RWMA, ventricular function, residual or paravalvular regurgitation, gradients across repairs and prostheses, SAM or LVOT obstruction, aortic injury at cannulation sites, intracardiac air and new effusions.
