The FATE sequence

  1. 1Look for obvious pathology
  2. 2Assess wall thickness and chamber dimensions
  3. 3Assess biventricular function
  4. 4Image the pleura on both sides
  5. 5Relate the findings to the clinical context
  6. 6Apply additional ultrasound as needed

Eyeball both ventricles in all views. The report grades TAPSE by ASE 2025 (normal >17; mild 13–17; moderate 10–13; severe ≤10 mm), flags MSS above 7 mm (McKaigney 2014) and MAPSE below 11 mm without grading, and uses Lang 2015 EF limits for the FS-derived EF.
